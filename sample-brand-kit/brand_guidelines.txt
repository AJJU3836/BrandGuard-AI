1. BRAND MISSION & VOICE
BrandGuard AI is the enterprise guardian of brand consistency, legal compliance,
and reputation protection. We empower creators while giving compliance teams 
transparent oversight.
- Tone: Professional, Empathetic, Objective, Authoritative, Clear.
- Avoid: Hyperbole, excessive emojis, sensationalism, aggressive sales pressure.

2. TERMINOLOGY & NAMING CONVENTIONS
- Official Product Name: "BrandGuard AI" (Note: Capital B, Capital G, space, capital AI).
- Approved Variations: "BrandGuard Governance Engine", "BrandGuard Platform".
- Banned Misspellings / Variants: "Brand Guard", "brandguard", "brand guard ai".
- Approved Vocabulary:
  * "Enterprise-grade"
  * "Pre-publication governance"
  * "Explainable AI findings"
  * "Grounded verification"
- Banned Vocabulary:
  * "cheap" (Use "cost-effective" or "enterprise-value")
  * "miracle"
  * "magic software"
  * "instant fix"

3. PROMOTIONAL CLAIMS & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE (ASCI Standards)
Following Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) directives:
- Do NOT make unsubstantiated superlative claims:
  * "#1 in India" is STRICTLY PROHIBITED unless backed by certified third-party market surveys.
  * "100% safe" or "100% error-free" is PROHIBITED. State actual benchmarked accuracy.
  * "Guaranteed results" is PROHIBITED.
- Approved Verified Claims & Facts:
  * "Over 500+ enterprise clients onboarded"
  * "Founded in 2024"
  * "ASCI pre-publication guideline compliant"
  * "ISO-27001 certified architecture"
